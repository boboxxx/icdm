# Verified research positioning — 2026-09-20

Only primary sources are used here; publication identifiers from the pasted proposal that have not been checked are not treated as verified references.

- Tong Wu et al., **ICDM: Interference Cancellation Diffusion Models for Wireless Semantic Communications**, arXiv:2505.19983 (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19983 . Uses signal/interference diffusion priors and a channel likelihood for cancellation. This is the reused baseline, not a contribution of RG-ICDM.
- Hyungjin Chung, Jeongsol Kim, Sehui Kim and Jong Chul Ye, **Parallel Diffusion Models of Operator and Image for Blind Inverse Problems**, CVPR 2023, pp. 6059–6069. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Chung_Parallel_Diffusion_Models_of_Operator_and_Image_for_Blind_Inverse_CVPR_2023_paper.html . Parallel operator/image priors already address blind inverse problems. Joint parameter estimation alone is not a defensible new claim.
- Benjamin Boys et al., **Tweedie Moment Projected Diffusions For Inverse Problems**, arXiv:2310.06721, version 3 (2024). https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06721 . Higher-order Tweedie information motivates principled conditional approximations. The old project's Gaussian-surrogate moments are not exact learned-score moments.
- Changyuan Zhao et al., **SecDiff: Diffusion-Aided Secure Deep Joint Source-Channel Coding Against Adversarial Attacks**, arXiv:2511.01466 (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01466 . Abstract verifies adaptive guidance, power-based subcarrier masking and alternating pilot recovery/channel estimation. Therefore adaptive guidance, power gates and alternating updates individually must not be claimed as unprecedented.
- Shunpu Tang et al., **Enabling Training-Free Semantic Communication Systems with Generative Diffusion Models**, arXiv:2505.01209 (2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01209 . Reuses diffusion through DDIM inversion/sampling and a split forward diffusion process. This differs from adapting an existing dual-prior interference canceller; training-free operation alone is not novel.

Provisional differentiation to test: receiver-only partial pooling across local interference estimates, explicit direct-decoder abstention, and separately controlled amplitude/guidance updates assessed by intervention harm and tail distortion at fixed neural evaluation budgets. This is a candidate contribution, not proof of novelty or empirical superiority. The residual gate provides measurement consistency only; it does not certify semantic fidelity.
